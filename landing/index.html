Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need a lot of documents to get started?

No. Start with whatever you have — a permit, a receipt, or your DTI paper. We tell you plainly what else would strengthen your credential, and you can add it later.

How much does it cost?

The basic credential is free for micro and small businesses. Partners pay for verification volume, not you.

Are my documents safe?

Everything is encrypted, and nothing is shared without your link. Every time someone opens your credential it appears in your log, and you can switch a link off anytime.

What if I don't have a laptop or fast internet?

The app is built phone-first and saves your work offline. When the signal returns, it uploads automatically.

Do banks accept this?

Yes — 17 banks, cooperatives and marketplaces already accept Trustlink credentials, and we add partners every month. You also get a printed copy if an office still asks for one.
